About us

Your local branding people.

West Orange Branding Company helps businesses and organizations get their name on apparel, promotional products, print and gifts.

Who you work with

Real people. One point of contact.

West Orange Branding Company is owned and run by MJ and Maka in Winter Garden. When you call or email, one of us answers, and the same two people see your order through from the first question to the finished box.

You may know us as West Orange Embroidery. Same people, with more to offer.

How we work

Made here, or sourced by us.

Made in-house

We run our own embroidery and laser engraving equipment and decorate most of that work ourselves.

Sourced for you

For everything else, and when a deadline calls for it, we work with suppliers and partner shops and manage the order for you.

Rooted in West Orange

We're based in Winter Garden and serve Horizon West, Ocoee, Windermere, Oakland, Clermont, Apopka and the rest of Central Florida. Farther out? We ship, so tell us about your project anyway.
